📖 How to use your memory book

  1. Start December 1 — open the 📅 December Days tab each evening and tap that day. Write one line about the day ("First snow! Built a snowman with Mia ❄️"). One line is enough — future-you will thank present-you.
  2. Add a photo — inside any day's memory page, tap "Choose photo" to attach one picture. Photos are stored right in the book on your device — no cloud, no account.
  3. Keep your traditions — the 🎄 Traditions tab lists classic family traditions. Tick each one as you do it and jot a note ("The lights drive was Leo's favourite part"). Add your own traditions at the bottom.
  4. Watch your streak grow — the 📊 Progress tab shows days recorded, photos, traditions kept, and your memory streak. Try not to break it — the book is best complete.
  5. After Christmas, write the review — open 💌 Year in Review on Boxing Day, fill in the highlights (favourite gifts, funniest moment, a letter to your future selves), and save it. This is the closing chapter.
  6. Export your keepsake — the ⬇️ Export tab downloads your whole memory book as one beautiful printable file to keep forever.
  7. Keep it on your phone — use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" so the book is one tap away. Your data stays on your device — private, offline, yours.
