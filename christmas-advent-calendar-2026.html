ℹ️ How to use your advent calendar

  1. Finish the Prep list in November — gather treats, check the ✅ Prep list tab, and edit any activities (⭐ Activities tab) to fit your family's traditions.
  2. From December 1, open one door each morning — tap the 🚪 Doors tab, open today's door, and read the family activity together (breakfast table works perfectly).
  3. Do the activity, then mark it done — tap "Mark as done ✓" inside the door. Your streak grows each consecutive day — kids love keeping it alive.
  4. Jot a note or memory — each door has a notes box. One line a day ("Mia loved the snowflake walk ❄️") becomes your family's December diary.
  5. Planning ahead? — switch on ✨ Planning mode on the Doors tab to peek at all 25 days early and shop for anything you need.
  6. Keep it on your phone — use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" so the calendar is one tap away every morning. Your data stays on your device — no account, no printing.
