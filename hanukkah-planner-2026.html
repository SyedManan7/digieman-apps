🕯️ Night-by-Night Menorah Checklist

📖 Blessing Reference

Recite before lighting — 2 every night, 3 on Night 1
1 · The lighting blessing — every night
“Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu melech ha’olam, asher kid’shanu b’mitzvotav, v’tzivanu l’hadlik ner shel Hanukkah.”
…who commanded us to kindle the Hanukkah light.
2 · The miracles blessing — every night
“Baruch atah Adonai … she’asah nissim la’avoteinu, bayamim haheim, bazman hazeh.”
…who performed miracles for our ancestors, in those days, at this time.
3 · Shehecheyanu — first night only
“Baruch atah Adonai … shehecheyanu, v’kiy’manu, v’higi’anu lazman hazeh.”
…who has kept us alive and brought us to this season.
After lighting, many families recite Hanerot Halalu and sing Ma’oz Tzur — mark them under each night with the toggles.

💡 Lighting Essentials

The shamash (helper candle) sits apart — a little higher or lower — and lights the others. New candles go on the left each night (right-to-left); lighting goes left-to-right, newest candle first.
Light at sundown; flames must burn at least 30 minutes after nightfall.
Display the menorah in a street-facing window or by the front door to publicise the miracle — and never leave burning candles unattended.

🌀 Dreidel Quick Rules

Everyone starts with equal tokens (gelt or chocolates)
נ
Nun — do nothing. The pot stays, play passes on.
ג
Gimel — take all the tokens in the pot. Everyone then adds one more to start a new pot.
ה
Hey — take half the pot (round up if odd).
ש
Shin — put one token into the pot. (In Israel the fourth side is Peh.)
Start each player with 10 tokens. When the pot runs low, everyone antes one more. Play until one player has them all! 🏆
