How spaced repetition works here

  1. Study a card, then grade yourself: Again (forgot), Hard, Good, Easy.
  2. Each grade feeds the SM-2 scheduler — the same algorithm behind SuperMemo & Anki.
  3. Cards you know come back later (days → weeks); cards you miss come back soon.
  4. Review a little every day — the 🔥 streak keeps you honest.
