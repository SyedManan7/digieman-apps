How Debt Freedom works

Three steps to your debt-free date.

  • Add each debt with its balance, APR and minimum payment.
  • Set an extra monthly payment — drag the slider to see years melt away.
  • Pick Snowball or Avalanche — then follow the battle plan each month and log payments as you go.
  • Everything is private: your numbers are stored only in this browser. No account, no internet needed after the first open.
  • Install it like an app: iPhone — Share → Add to Home Screen. Android — menu → Add to Home screen. Desktop — bookmark the file.
